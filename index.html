01 — The problem

A doctor's most valuable resource is time with the patient.

Too much of the consultation is spent reconstructing a story that was never organized in the first place. ClinSync moves that work before the consultation.

01

Repeated questions

Basic history is re-asked at every visit because nothing carries forward.

02

Manual document review

Prescriptions, labs and discharge summaries are read one by one, by hand.

03

Scattered records

Years of history live across files that never speak to each other.

04

Fragmented information

Nothing is organized around the single question that matters: why today?

05

Repetitive documentation

Notes are written from scratch after every consultation.

→

ClinSync changes this

Collection, organization and preparation move to the patient side, before the door opens.

The core philosophy

ClinSync is not an AI doctor. It is an AI preparation and information organization layer around the doctor — the doctor remains the final authority.

Built so the clinician decides, with every claim traceable to its source.

08 — Trust & safety

Clinical data is not ordinary application data.

Privacy by design, at every layer of the platform.

Role-based access

Patient, Doctor, Admin. Authorization happens at the backend — never trusted from the frontend.

Explicit consent

Scoped, time-bound consent checked against authorization rules on every sensitive access.

Audit logs

Every sensitive action recorded — without storing unnecessary sensitive metadata.

Fail gracefully

AI failure never destroys or hides original patient information. The doctor is never blocked.

Observable AI

Latency, token usage, status and validation results tracked — without logging raw medical data.

Provider abstraction

LLM, speech, OCR and embedding providers are swappable — no single-vendor lock-in.
